About

At Telecom International Myanmar (Mytel), I moved from coordinating store operations to heading the store-channel division. The work ran on numbers: building Power BI dashboards for directors and C-level executives, analysing sales performance and sales quality, benchmarking our prices against competitors, setting targets and writing the management reports that shaped commercial decisions. I also coordinated store managers, franchisees and distributors, so I’m used to explaining data to people who need to act on it.

I’m now building the technical side in Melbourne through postgraduate study in analytics, AI and cloud platforms, working with Power BI, Azure Data Factory, Azure SQL and Databricks.
